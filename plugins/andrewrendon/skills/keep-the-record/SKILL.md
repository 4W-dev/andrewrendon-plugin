---
name: keep-the-record
description: Records what the owner says about work their Andrew Rendon setup did for them. Use whenever the owner mentions what they did with drafts a job made (sent them, sent them untouched, changed or rewrote them, deleted or binned them), corrects how something should be done, or says something about the business has changed. Use it even when it is an aside or an FYI, or comes alongside another request.
---

When the owner says what happened to a job's drafts, call the andrewrendon connector's `record_verdict` tool with the job and three counts: sent as written, changed first, not sent. Work the counts out from what they said. Ask only if the numbers cannot be worked out.

When the owner corrects how something should be done, or says a rule or a fact about the business has changed, call the andrewrendon connector's `record_correction` tool with their exact words. Then do what its reply asks: when the business keeps a company brain, the reply says which documents to update, and the update is part of recording it.

Then say in one line what was recorded, and carry on with anything else they asked for.

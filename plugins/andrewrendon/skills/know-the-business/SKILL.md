---
name: know-the-business
description: Reads how this business works before writing or answering anything for it. Use for any email, reply, note, post, bio, checklist, job ad, out-of-office, idea or answer that is for the business, its clients, customers or team, or that names one of them.
---

Before you write or answer, call the andrewrendon connector's `get_business` tool once in the conversation, unless you already have its result.

Then:
- Follow the owner's rulings. They win over everything else, including your own judgment about what reads better.
- Write the way the business notes say the business sounds, and sign the way they say.
- Use the people, clients and facts from the notes by name. If the notes don't cover something the answer needs, such as a price or a date, say that it isn't written down rather than inventing it.
